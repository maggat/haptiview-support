HaptiView privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. HaptiView is made by Studio Rosell.

Summary

HaptiView does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (intensity, sync offset, keep-screen-on), the last session address and the last web address you opened are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Web pages you open

The built-in web player loads the sites you choose, like any browser. Those sites may collect data under their own privacy policies. HaptiView does not read or send your browsing to us.

Local network and camera

If you connect to a desktop session, HaptiView talks directly to that device on your local network. The camera is used only to scan a session QR code; no images are stored or sent.

Children

HaptiView is not directed at children.
