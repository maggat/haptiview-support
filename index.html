HaptiView support

HaptiView is a web video player for iPhone that turns a video's haptic track into smooth vibration, in sync with playback. Made by Studio Rosell.

Getting started

  1. Open HaptiView and tap Open web player.
  2. Tap the demo icon in the bottom bar to try the built-in demo, or type a site address.
  3. Tap Start (top right) once. iPhone needs one tap before the motor may run.
  4. Play the video. Pause, seek or leave the page and the vibration stops.

Troubleshooting

  • No vibration: check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics is on, and that you tapped Start. Try Test pulse on the home screen.
  • Out of sync: use the sync offset slider (±500 ms) on the home screen.
  • Too strong or weak: use the intensity slider.
  • Haptics play only while HaptiView is open on screen; iOS does not allow the haptic motor in the background.

For site developers

Inside HaptiView every page gets window.PulseLink.send({type:'sample', t, i}), where t is the video time in ms and i is the intensity from 0 to 1. navigator.vibrate() works too. Send 20–40 samples per second while the video plays; the app stops the motor on pause, seek and navigation.
